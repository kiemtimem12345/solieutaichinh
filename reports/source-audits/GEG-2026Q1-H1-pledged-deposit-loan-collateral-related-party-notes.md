## 1. Restricted bank deposits — three distinct classifications
- H1 p11 B01 code165 short-term other assets: **54,079,653,068** at 30 June 2026, **11,422,218,903** as restated Jan 1 2026. This same line is not separately presented as code165 on the Q1 original B01 p6, whose short-term other assets show codes161–163 only (Q1 p6 PDF).
- H1 p44 **Note22**: expressly states other current assets include deposits whose use is restricted by loan agreements, held to secure borrowings and released when contractual conditions are fulfilled. The narrative does **not** name their banking accounts, amounts per contract or 31 March status.
- H1 p73 **Note37**: FY-open reclassification, under TT99/2025, exactly 11,422,218,903 from cash equivalents/total cash line to code165 as shown; explains beginning-of-year comparator, not the March 31 restricted deposit balance. Note37 also separately moves **22,420,000,000** of related-party short-term loan receivable to held-to-maturity investments and **19,907,831,170** from other receivables to held-to-maturity. These are reclassifications of different asset classes, not extra collateral deposits.
- Q1 p22 **Note5**: 31 March cash and equivalents **105,856,793,049**, consisting bank deposits 46,625,510,309 and equivalents 59,231,282,740. There is no itemized March restricted bank deposit amount in this note.
- H1 p35 **Note5** specifically describes 30 June cash and equivalents as balances **not restricted in use**: bank demand deposits **54,471,151,057**, cash on hand 16,052,000, cash equivalents 45,835,030,271; total **100,322,233,328**. Its opening cash and equivalents is **74,942,444,345**, restated.
- The annual maturity 6–12-month bank deposits in H1 **Note6.2 pp36–37** total **351,626,527,874** at 30 June (including Vietcombank Gia Lai **336,500,000,000**) and Q1 original **Note6 PDF p23** shows 6–12-month deposits **288,087,676,704** at 31 March. These belong to financial investments, not cash equivalents; collateral-pledged status of any specific such account cannot be inferred from aggregate maturity table.

These are future contributions and NOT pledged bank deposits or the 11,422,218,903 cash presentation adjustment. Note35.1 lease commitments similarly are not cash balances.

## 3. Loans / associates / subsidiaries — do not conflate
- H1 p36 **Note6.1**: short-term loan to **Công ty Cổ phần Giao dịch Hàng hóa Sơn Tín** principal **22,420,000,000**, contractual interest 9.5–10%, unsecured, **fully provisioned**. Long past due; note states 2025 board-approved full allowance and borrower had not fulfilled payment obligations at H1 issuance. This is a loan receivable to a **related party of a major shareholder** (H1 p65 Note33), NOT a pledged deposit nor a loan to a consolidated subsidiary. H1 p68 Note33 shows 22,420,000,000 principal and 14,439,427,395 interest fully provisioned in the held-to-maturity/related-party table.
- H1 p42 **Note17**: **Công ty Cổ phần Năng lượng Solwind** is an associate, with equity-accounted investment **56,008,966,262** at June 30; not bank deposits nor evidence of loans to subsidiaries.
- H1 pp64–68 **Note33 related-party transactions**: balances and sales/fees to various related-party entities; 2025 comparatives show loan repayments from parties such as Sơn Tín / Thủy điện Trường Phú, but no 2026 H1 loan recoveries were listed for those rows. The transactions and related-party receivables are distinct from group cash under pledge.
- Loans between controlled entities within the consolidated group would be eliminated on consolidation; do not interpret an investment in a subsidiary or intra-group loan from a **separate/parent** report as a consolidated third-party bank deposit.
